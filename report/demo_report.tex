\section{Equation and reference}
The model equation is the Poisson equation in 1D:
\begin{equation}
  -\frac{d^2 u}{dx^2} = f(x), \qquad x\in(0,1),
  \label{eq:poisson}
\end{equation}
with boundary conditions $u(0)=u_0$ and $u'(1)=g$. Reference the equation with \verb|\ref{eq:poisson}|.
